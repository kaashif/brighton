Kaashif vs Brando McCready

For King & Comp Play · Leagues of Votann

★★☆☆☆2/5 for Kaashif

The Votann list combines two large Hearthguard bricks with Thunderkyn, fast Pioneers and numerous Yaegir screens.

High confidence. Based on the submitted Brighton list and current 11th-edition datasheets.

What the army does

Tough mid-range infantry and vehicles with efficient high-damage shooting and strong objective control.

What they probably try

They usually establish a shooting castle, mark priority targets and move durable units onto the midfield once trades begin.

Kaashif’s plan

Avoid long lanes and expose only when Wraiths can connect. Their D3+ and rail-style weapons are much worse for C’tan than ordinary D2 volume.

Protect C’tan from grav fire, do not let the Yaegirs pull the army apart and use Wraiths to pin the mobile elements before committing into Hearthguard.

List-specific traps

No exact datasheet audit is possible without a usable submitted list. Check the opponent’s roster at the table.

How to read the rating
