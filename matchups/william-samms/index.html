Kaashif vs William Samms

Wessex Wyverns · Blood Angels

★★★☆☆3/5 for Kaashif

The Blood Angels melee is welcome, but two Vindicators, speeders and haywire add serious high-damage shooting and mortal wounds.

High confidence. Based on the submitted Brighton list and current 11th-edition datasheets.

What the army does

Fast Space Marine melee pressure backed by jump units, transports and a smaller shooting package.

What they probably try

They threaten an early multi-charge, use speed to choose a flank and try to kill support characters during the scrum.

Kaashif’s plan

This is broadly the kind of matchup Kaashif wants: receive the first wave with Wraiths, protect the Technomancers, then counter-charge with the missile.

Hide C’tan from Vindicator lanes, protect Technomancers from the Scout sniper and counter-charge jump units only after the shooting pieces are pinned or screened.

How to read the rating

★★★★★ strongly favourable · ★★★☆☆ roughly even · ★☆☆☆☆ strongly unfavourable. This is a subjective pairing read for Kaashif’s submitted Necron list, not a player or army power rating.

The read rewards fast melee that Wraiths can absorb and the missile can counter-charge. It penalises fast shooting, Precision into Technomancers, mortal/Devastating Wound output and D3+ weapons into C’tan. Ordinary Damage 2 volume is comparatively welcome because Necrodermis reduces it efficiently.
