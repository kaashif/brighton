Kaashif vs Daniel Fuller

Defenders of Outpost 47 · Emperor's Children

★★★☆☆3/5 for Kaashif

The army comes into melee, but widespread Precision plus Fulgrim’s high damage and mortal wounds directly attack the Necron support structure.

High confidence. Based on the submitted Brighton list and current 11th-edition datasheets.

What the army does

Fast elite melee pressure with numerous character-led units, Precision access and powerful monsters.

What they probably try

They want to dictate combats, isolate leaders and use Fulgrim or another hammer to break the durable centre.

Kaashif’s plan

Receive the charge through Wraiths, never offer a clean Precision line to a Technomancer and counter-charge the committed hammer with several threats together.

Keep Technomancers out of character lines, let Wraiths receive lesser units and counter Fulgrim only with multiple missile elements together.
