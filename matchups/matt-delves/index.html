Kaashif’s plan

Stay off every rail/beam lane, push Wraiths into the vehicles together and protect Technomancers from any Preymark or other Precision angle.

How to read the rating

★★★★★ strongly favourable · ★★★☆☆ roughly even · ★☆☆☆☆ strongly unfavourable. This is a subjective pairing read for Kaashif’s submitted Necron list, not a player or army power rating.

The read rewards fast melee that Wraiths can absorb and the missile can counter-charge. It penalises fast shooting, Precision into Technomancers, mortal/Devastating Wound output and D3+ weapons into C’tan. Ordinary Damage 2 volume is comparatively welcome because Necrodermis reduces it efficiently.
