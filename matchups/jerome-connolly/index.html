Kaashif vs Jerome Connolly

Comp Play Surrey Primarchs · Adeptus Mechanicus

★★☆☆☆2/5 for Kaashif

AdMech combine firing lanes, vehicle-killing guns and genuine Precision into the support package.

High confidence. Based on the submitted Brighton list and current 11th-edition datasheets.

What the army does

A combined-arms gunline with efficient vehicles, specialist infantry and layered targeting support.

What they probably try

They try to establish firing lanes, trade disposable screens and focus a key target with high-damage guns.

Kaashif’s plan

Deny lanes, do not drip-feed C’tan into anti-tank fire, and use Wraiths to pin the shooting pieces before exposing the missile.

Stay completely hidden until Wraiths can tag several guns; never expose a Technomancer to the Rangers and do not send a C’tan alone into the D6+ damage.
